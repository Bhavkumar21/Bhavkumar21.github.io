About

My research program is on the dynamics of foundation model training in regimes where standard theoretical assumptions fail. Classical learning theory presumes infinite width, deterministic gradient flow, and verifiable reward signals, but the models we deploy operate at finite width, are trained with stochastic optimization at large learning rates, and increasingly on non-verifiable tasks. I take the gap between these idealized regimes and actual training to be the central object of study, and I work on building theory and methods that are faithful to the conditions under which modern models are trained.

The work I am drawn to is mathematically honest and practically sharp, the kind that will still matter in ten years. Work like that is rarer than it should be, and I have come to believe it is mostly a question of taste, built slowly and meticulously. I want to spend my career building mine.
